Here is the latest version of my "Polar Align Mount" app, created using artificial intelligence (Claude AI).

It allows you to use your phone's GPS and gyroscope to polar align an equatorial mount. The principle is to place your smartphone flat on the mount's base plate, with the mount in a horizontal position (latitude = 0).

After activating the phone's sensors (button at the bottom of the app; you must allow the use of location and motion sensors), you first align the mount horizontally along the east-west axis (step 1), then along the north-south axis (step 2). You can go back to check the alignment. Next, you set the altitude (step 3) and then the north azimuth (step 4).
You also find all the necessary elements (GPS, level, altitude, and compass) as well as a Skywatcher-style reticle if you wish to adjust the alignment manually without the assistant.

A menu is also available, with three possible settings:

1. Day/night mode,

2. Level calibration to ±2° in 0.1° increments,

3. Selection of the correction method for converting Magnetic North Pole to Geographic North Pole (auto: based on NOAA's WMM2025, manual: if the precise value for your location is known, or no correction: retains the Magnetic North Pole).



This version works on both iOS and Android phones. It is a PWA (Progressive Web App) that you can add to your home screen like an app downloaded from an app store.

On iPhone with Safari:

1. Open your app's URL in Safari (not Chrome or Firefox)

2. Tap the Share button—the square with an upward arrow—at the bottom of the screen

3. Scroll down the list of actions and tap "Add to Home Screen"

4. Change the name if you wish (default is "Polar Align"), then tap "Add" in the top right corner


On Android with Chrome:

1. Open the URL in Chrome (recommended) or Edge

2. Tap the menu ⋮ (three dots) in the top right corner

3. Tap "Add to Home Screen"

4. Confirm the name and then tap "Add"



Hope you enjoy this app.
